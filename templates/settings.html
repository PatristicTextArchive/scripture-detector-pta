Model Selection

Gemini limits how many requests a model accepts per minute (free tiers often only 5–15). Long texts are analysed in parts of about 12,000 characters, one request each. The app waits between requests and retries automatically when the limit is hit. 0 = no limit.

Bible Text Search (RAG)

Text search only: no model is used. Passages that reuse a verse closely (full or partial quotations, about six words or more) are found by comparing the text with the Bible texts. Paraphrases and allusions need a model.

When unchecked, the model works on its own (no candidate verses, nothing added, corrected or dropped afterwards). Not relevant for Text search only.
Controls how closely a passage must follow a verse to be offered to the model as a candidate, to be added without the model, and to count as a verbatim quotation. Very short phrases (two to four words) and common phrases that occur in many verses are filtered out; strict asks for more words and rarer phrases.
After the model has answered: passages that clearly reuse a verse are added even if the model overlooked them; a reference the model got wrong is replaced by the verse the passage actually matches; results that are only a very short or common phrase are dropped. Uncheck to use the Bible texts only as candidates in the prompt.
At most this many (default 50 for Gemini, 25 for local models; fewer if few verses match well enough). The prompt must fit into the context window. Leave empty for the default.

Text Parts

Long texts are sent in parts. Default: about 12,000 for Gemini and 3,500 for local models (Greek needs many tokens per word: keep the part plus the answer within the model's context window). Leave empty for the default.
